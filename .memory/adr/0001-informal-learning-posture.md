# ADR 0001: Informal Learning Posture (Not Course-Building)

## Status: Accepted (2026-08-08)

## Context

After implementing GDQuest-inspired pedagogical spikes (problem-first openings, prediction quizzes, scaffold withdrawal), user feedback indicated we were overfitting to course-building patterns. Our JTBD is "understand well enough to do my job" — informal, mission-driven learning alongside delivery, not structured coursework.

## Decision

The teach skill's posture is **knowledgeable colleague at a whiteboard**, not **course instructor performing a lesson**. Specifically:

- No narrative framing ("picture this" scenarios) — brief context, then teach
- No prediction quizzes or scaffold withdrawal — those are course patterns
- Exercises test fundamental understanding ("explain why"), not mechanical recall
- The Socratic gate is quiz-me (an existing skill), not a new mechanism
- Reading level Grade 8-10 for clarity, not for a target audience

## Consequences

- Discarded spikes: 025 (prediction quiz), 027 (scaffold withdrawal)
- Simplified spikes: 020 (opening), 022 (callbacks), 024 (exercises), 026 (checklist)
- Teach skill guidance is shorter and less prescriptive
- GDQuest research still useful (reference docs, details pattern, reading level) — but applied selectively
