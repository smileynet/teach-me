# Teaching Notes

## Learner Profile
- Consulting context: learning to advise a customer, not just for self
- Needs to speak credibly to both producer and consumer teams
- Prefers practical/buildable over theoretical
- Comfortable with AWS at a high level — wants to go deeper on specifics as needed
- Conceptual foundation in data engineering exists — don't over-explain basics

## Preferences
- Start small, scaffold up
- Wants to understand limitations and operational concerns, not just happy-path setup
