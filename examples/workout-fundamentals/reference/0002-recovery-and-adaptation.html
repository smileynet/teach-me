Recovery & Adaptation

Reference · Companion to Lesson 2

Core Concept

Training is the stimulus; adaptation happens during rest. Sleep and protein are the two highest-leverage factors. Everything else is optimization.

Recovery Priority

FactorTargetWhy
Sleep7-9 hours/night70% of GH secreted during deep sleep. Non-negotiable.
Protein1.6-2.2 g/kg/dayAmino acids for repair. 20-40g per meal, 3-5 meals.
CaloriesAt or above maintenanceDeficit impairs recovery — body prioritizes survival over muscle.
HydrationDrink to thirst + extra around training2% dehydration impairs performance.
Stress managementReduce where possibleCortisol is catabolic. Life stress + training stress share a budget.

DOMS vs Fatigue

Muscle SorenessSystemic Fatigue
LocationSpecific musclesGlobal — everything
MeaningNovel stimulus (not quality indicator)Accumulated under-recovery
ActionTrain normallyDeload or extra rest

Overreaching Signs

  • Stalled lifts 2+ sessions (despite sleep/nutrition)
  • Weights feel heavier than they should
  • Persistent joint ache (not muscle soreness)
  • Poor sleep despite being tired
  • Declining motivation
  • Getting sick more often

Decision Aid

"Should I train today?"

  • Mild muscle soreness → yes, train normally
  • Severe soreness (painful to touch, can't move normally) → wait one more day
  • Everything feels heavy + poor sleep + declining motivation → deload week
  • Joint pain (sharp, in the joint itself) → see a professional

Deload Protocol

  1. Keep exercises the same (maintain motor patterns)
  2. Reduce volume 40-60% (fewer sets)
  3. Keep intensity moderate (don't go too light)
  4. Duration: 5-7 days
  5. Frequency: every 4-6 weeks of progressive training

Supercompensation Timing

  • Same muscle group: wait 48-72 hours
  • Full-body training: 3×/week (M/W/F) fits this naturally
  • Train in the window, not before recovery completes
