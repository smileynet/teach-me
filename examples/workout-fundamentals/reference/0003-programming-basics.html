Programming Basics

Reference · Companion to Lesson 3

Core Concept

3 full-body sessions per week, 5 compound movements each, add weight when you hit your reps. That's it for 3-9 months.

Linear Progression Rules

  1. Pick a rep target (e.g., 3 × 8-12)
  2. Hit all sets at top of range with good form → add 2.5kg next session
  3. Can't complete minimum reps (3 × 8) → repeat same weight
  4. Stall 2+ sessions → check sleep/food first, then deload
  5. Continue until session-to-session progress stops (3-9 months)

Decision Aid

"Should I do a split or full-body?"

  • Training <1 year → full-body 3×/wk (more practice, simpler, miss-proof)
  • Can't fit enough volume into 3 sessions → upper/lower 4×/wk
  • Enjoy training 5-6 days → split is fine (results are identical per 2024 meta-analysis)

"Am I doing enough volume?"

  • Making progress → enough, don't add more
  • Stalled but recovering well → try adding 1-2 sets/muscle/week
  • Stalled AND fatigued → too much, deload then restart at lower volume

What Doesn't Matter (Yet)

ThingReality
Exercise selectionAny exercise overloading the target works. Pick what you'll do consistently.
PeriodizationLinear is optimal for novices. Complex schemes solve intermediate problems.
Failure trainingRIR 1-3 = same results, less fatigue and injury risk.
Machines vs free weightsEquivalent hypertrophy. Both belong in a program.
Exact rest timing90s to 3min all work. Longer is slightly better for compounds.
