# Mission

## Why I'm learning this

I want to start strength training consistently and make informed decisions about my programming — not follow influencer advice blindly. I've been sedentary and want to understand the science well enough to know when advice is evidence-based vs bro-science.

## What success looks like

I can design a simple, evidence-based training program for myself. I understand progressive overload, recovery, and volume well enough to explain WHY my program is structured the way it is. When someone gives me gym advice, I can evaluate whether it's supported by research.

## Context

- No training background — completely sedentary
- Interested in general strength and health, not bodybuilding competition
- Access to a basic gym (barbell, dumbbells, machines)
- Skeptical of fitness industry claims — wants evidence, not hype
- Understands this is a long game (years, not weeks)
